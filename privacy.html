Information we collect

When you book an appointment, send a message or subscribe to our newsletter, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, date of birth and insurance provider.

We also collect limited technical information, such as browser type and pages visited, to keep the website secure and working well.

How we use information

  • To schedule and confirm appointments and send reminders
  • To respond to your questions
  • To verify insurance benefits
  • To send newsletters you've subscribed to (you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • To improve our website and services

We never sell your personal information.

HIPAA notice of privacy practices

Your protected health information (PHI) is handled in accordance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act. A full Notice of Privacy Practices is available at our front desk or on request.

Please don't send detailed medical information through the website contact form. Call us instead.

Cookies

We use essential cookies and local storage to remember your preferences (for example, your cookie choice). With your consent, we may use analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our site.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, and you may opt out of marketing communications at any time.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit, access controls and staff training, to protect your information.

Terms of use

Content on this website is for general information only and is not a substitute for professional dental or medical advice. Online booking requests are confirmed subject to availability. Prices shown are starting prices and may vary based on your individual treatment plan.

Links to other websites

We may link to third-party sites, such as maps and social media. We aren't responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Accessibility statement

We aim for our website to meet WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines. It supports keyboard navigation, screen readers, reduced motion preferences and resizable text. Our office is wheelchair accessible, with step-free entry, an elevator and accessible restrooms.

If you have trouble using any part of this site, please let us know.
