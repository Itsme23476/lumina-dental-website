Our journey

From two chairs to a full-service clinic

Every milestone came from listening to what our patients needed next.

2008

Lumina opens its doors

Dr. Amelia Hart opens a two-chair family practice on Harbor Avenue.

2012

Cosmetic dentistry arrives

Dr. Marcus Chen joins, bringing veneers, whitening and digital smile design.

2016

A bigger home

We move into Suite 200 with eight treatment rooms, a kids' corner and free parking.

2019

Specialists under one roof

Orthodontics, oral surgery and endodontics join, so patients rarely need referrals.

2023

Fully digital

3D cone-beam imaging, intraoral scanners and same-day treatment planning.

2026

12,000+ patients and counting

Still family-owned, still focused on comfort, and still welcoming new patients.
