Your first visit

What to expect (about 90 minutes)

Warm welcome

Check in, enjoy a coffee and meet your patient coordinator.

Digital imaging

Low-radiation X-rays and photos so we can see the full picture.

Exam & cleaning

A comprehensive exam, gum health check and gentle cleaning.

Your plan

We review findings together and give you a written cost estimate.

What to bring

  • Photo ID and your insurance card
  • A list of current medications and allergies
  • Completed new patient forms (or arrive 15 minutes early)
  • Recent X-rays from a previous dentist, if available

Feeling nervous?

You're not alone. Tell us when you book and we'll schedule extra time, explain each step before we do it and agree on a "stop" signal. Nitrous oxide and oral sedation are available.
